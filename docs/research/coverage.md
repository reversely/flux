# Source coverage by tile

Every item in the twelve tiles, the form it takes, and whether its instructions have a sourced
origin. An item is **ready** when a named openly licensed source supplies its nodes, **partial**
when the steps or characters exist but the reference figures or per-item values do not, and
**open** when no source has been verified yet.

The detail lives in three briefs: `process-node-sources.md` for process steps and figures,
`synoptic-characters-fungi-plants.md` and `synoptic-characters-animals.md` for identification
characters. This page is the index; those pages carry the evidence and the licence verdicts.

Two rules govern every row. This project produces no datasets, so an item with no existing open
source ships without camera judgement. Where FM 21-76 diverges from current guidance, the node
carries current guidance and names the divergence (PRD 6.5.7).

## What blocks the most rows

Figures block fewer items than first thought. Forty Commons files cite FM 21-76 and all forty are
public domain, covering the shelter, fire, water, and direction-finding procedures; the audit is on
#137. What remains true is that most plates show a finished result rather than one step, so a plate
usually grounds a whole procedure while individual nodes still need their own art, and four items
(the fallout shelter most sharply) still lack usable art. Steps, by contrast, exist for every process item; what most of them lack is a
statement of what completion looks like, which only the mountaineering and task-checklist manuals
provide.
